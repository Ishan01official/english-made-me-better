# Sentence Practice

## 1. Why this matters

Sentence Practice helps you write clear and useful messages. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Sentence Practice is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Sentence Practice teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Worknote: Checked logs and found no recent failures.
- Email: Could you please share the required logs?
- Ticket update: Waiting for customer confirmation.
- - Please share the required logs.
- - Could you please confirm the Azure subscription ID?

## 6. Common mistakes

- Please revert back. -> Please reply.
- Kindly do the needful. -> Please take the required action.
- I am having a doubt. -> I have a question.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Please revert back.
  Correct: Please reply.
- Incorrect: Kindly do the needful.
  Correct: Please take the required action.
- Incorrect: I am having a doubt.
  Correct: I have a question.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Rewrite a ticket update professionally.
2. Write an email asking for logs.
3. Summarize a technical issue in 3 sentences.

## 10. Answer key

1. Use clear action and status.
2. Could you please share the required logs?
3. State issue, action taken, and next step.

## 11. Feynman exercise

Explain this topic in your own simple words.

Use this frame:

- The topic is...
- It matters because...
- A simple example is...
- One mistake I must avoid is...

## 12. Daily drill

Write 5 original sentences. Read them aloud. Correct one mistake. Then teach the rule in 3 simple lines.
