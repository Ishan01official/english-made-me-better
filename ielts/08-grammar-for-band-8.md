# Grammar For IELTS Band 8

## What Band 8 Grammar Means

You need variety and accuracy. Complex grammar is useful only when it is controlled.

## Structures To Master

| Structure | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Complex sentence | Although technology improves convenience, it can reduce face-to-face communication. |
| Relative clause | People who live in large cities often face higher living costs. |
| Conditional | If governments invest in public transport, congestion may decrease. |
| Passive voice | More funding should be allocated to public health. |
| Comparison | Online learning is more flexible than traditional classroom learning. |
| Concession | While this policy may be expensive, it could bring long-term benefits. |
