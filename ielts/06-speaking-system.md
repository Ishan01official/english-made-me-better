# IELTS Speaking System

## Goal

Speak naturally, clearly, and with enough detail for Band 8.

## Test Parts

| Part | Time | What Happens | Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Part 1 | 4-5 min | familiar questions | Answer + reason + small example |
| Part 2 | 3-4 min | cue card and 2-minute talk | Use story structure |
| Part 3 | 4-5 min | abstract discussion | Give opinion, reason, example, contrast |

## Part 1 Formula

```text
Direct answer + reason + small example
```

Example:

> Yes, I enjoy reading because it helps me relax. For example, I usually read short articles after work.

## Part 2 Formula

```text
Past -> Description -> Why it mattered -> Reflection
```

## Part 3 Formula

```text
Opinion -> Reason -> Example -> Alternative view
```

## Daily Speaking Drill

1. Answer 5 Part 1 questions.
2. Record 1 Part 2 cue card.
3. Answer 3 Part 3 questions.
4. Note grammar and pronunciation mistakes.
5. Repeat the weakest answer.
