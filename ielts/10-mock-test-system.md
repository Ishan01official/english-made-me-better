# Mock Test System

## When To Start Full Mocks

Start full mocks from Day 45. Before that, build skill foundations.

## Weekly Mock Format

1. Listening: 30 minutes
2. Reading: 60 minutes
3. Writing: 60 minutes
4. Speaking: record a full 11-14 minute test

## Mock Review

Do not just record the score. Record why you lost marks.

## Score Stability Rule

Before booking the exam, aim for:

- Listening: 35+ in 3 mocks
- Reading: 35+ in 3 mocks
- Writing: at least 7.5 level in repeated self-checks or tutor review
- Speaking: fluent 12-minute recording with few repeated errors
