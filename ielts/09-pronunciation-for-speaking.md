# Pronunciation For IELTS Speaking

## Goal

Be easy to understand, natural, and controlled. You do not need a British or American accent.

## Band 8 Pronunciation Focus

- clear individual sounds
- word stress
- sentence stress
- intonation
- chunking
- natural pace

## Daily Drill

1. Read one answer aloud.
2. Mark stressed words.
3. Record once slowly.
4. Record once naturally.
5. Listen for unclear sounds.
