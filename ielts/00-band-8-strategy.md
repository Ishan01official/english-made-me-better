# Band 8 Strategy

## 1. First Principles

IELTS is not a grammar exam. It is a performance exam.

You must train three things:

1. English skill
2. IELTS task skill
3. Time pressure control

If you only study English, you may still lose marks because you do not know the task. If you only do test tricks, your language will not reach Band 8. You need both.

## 2. Band 8 Score Logic

To reach overall Band 8, aim higher than the minimum:

| Skill | Training Target |
| --- | --- |
| Listening | 36/40 in practice |
| Reading | 36/40 in practice |
| Writing | consistent 7.5-8 level structure |
| Speaking | natural, extended, accurate answers |

## 3. 150-Day Method

| Phase | Days | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 1-30 | Understand format, build routine, fix basic grammar and pronunciation |
| Skill Build | 31-75 | Learn question types, writing structures, speaking answer systems |
| Band 8 Upgrade | 76-120 | Timed practice, high-level vocabulary, argument quality, fluency |
| Exam Simulation | 121-150 | Full mocks, error reduction, test-day strategy |

## 4. What Not To Do

- Do not memorize essays.
- Do not watch videos without producing answers.
- Do not ignore Writing Task 1.
- Do not practice speaking silently.
- Do not do mocks without error analysis.
- Do not book the exam until your mock scores are stable.

## 5. Weekly Score Review

Every Sunday, record:

- Listening score out of 40
- Reading score out of 40
- Writing weak criterion
- Speaking weak criterion
- top 10 repeated mistakes
- next week target
