# Diagnostic Test

Complete this in the first 2 days.

## Step 1: Listening

Use an official IELTS sample listening test. Take it under test conditions.

Record:

- score out of 40
- question types missed
- spelling errors
- number/date errors
- distractor errors

## Step 2: Reading

Use an official IELTS Academic or General Training reading sample based on your target test.

Record:

- score out of 40
- time per passage
- question types missed
- unknown vocabulary
- careless mistakes

## Step 3: Writing

Write:

- Task 1 in 20 minutes
- Task 2 in 40 minutes

Self-check using:

- Did I answer the task?
- Are paragraphs clear?
- Did I use examples?
- Did I repeat words too much?
- Did I make grammar errors?

## Step 4: Speaking

Record:

- Part 1: 5 short answers
- Part 2: 2-minute long turn
- Part 3: 5 discussion answers

Check:

- fluency
- grammar mistakes
- pronunciation clarity
- vocabulary range
- answer development
