# IELTS Band 8 Track

Target: minimum overall IELTS Band 8 within 150 days.

This section is a practical IELTS training system. It focuses on the four tested skills:

- Listening
- Reading
- Writing
- Speaking

The target is not only to learn English. The target is to perform under IELTS timing, format, and scoring pressure.

## Band 8 Reality

Band 8 means very good command of English. Official IELTS scoring guidance describes Band 8 as strong operational command with only occasional unsystematic inaccuracies and good handling of complex argumentation. That means your preparation must focus on:

- accuracy
- speed
- structure
- topic vocabulary
- error reduction
- full mock tests

## Daily Minimum

If you have only 60 minutes:

1. 15 min listening
2. 15 min reading
3. 20 min writing or speaking
4. 10 min error log

If you have 120 minutes:

1. 25 min listening
2. 25 min reading
3. 35 min writing
4. 25 min speaking
5. 10 min error log

## Weekly Non-Negotiables

- 2 Listening timed sections
- 2 Reading timed passages
- 2 Writing Task 2 essays
- 2 Writing Task 1 reports or letters
- 3 Speaking recordings
- 1 full mock test from Day 45 onward
- 1 error-log review
