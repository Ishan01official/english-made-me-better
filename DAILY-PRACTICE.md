# Daily Practice

Use this file as the daily operating system for English practice.

## 30-Minute Plan

- 5 min pronunciation: one sound, one stress pattern, or one drill.
- 10 min grammar: one small rule and 5 original examples.
- 5 min vocabulary: 5 useful words with one sentence each.
- 5 min sentence practice: correct 3 sentences.
- 5 min speaking: speak for 60 seconds and repeat once.

## 60-Minute Plan

- 10 min pronunciation
- 15 min grammar
- 10 min vocabulary
- 10 min writing
- 10 min speaking
- 5 min review

## 120-Minute Plan

- 20 min pronunciation
- 25 min grammar
- 20 min reading
- 20 min writing
- 20 min speaking
- 15 min review and correction

## Daily Checklist

- [ ] I learned one rule.
- [ ] I wrote original examples.
- [ ] I corrected at least one mistake.
- [ ] I spoke aloud.
- [ ] I explained the topic simply.
