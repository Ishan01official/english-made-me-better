# Speaking Deep Practice: 120 Scenarios

Speaking improves when you repeat useful situations until they become natural.

## First Principles Of Speaking

Speaking has four layers:

1. Thought: what do I want to say?
2. Structure: what order should I say it in?
3. Sentence: what exact words should I use?
4. Sound: how clearly do I pronounce it?

## Simple Speaking Structure

```text
Point -> Reason -> Example -> Close
```

Example:

> I think daily speaking practice is important. It makes grammar active, not only theoretical. For example, when I record myself, I notice repeated mistakes. That is why I practice speaking every day.

## How To Practice Each Scenario

1. Read the model answer.
2. Close the file.
3. Speak your own version.
4. Record it.
5. Listen for grammar, clarity, pronunciation, and confidence.
6. Repeat once with corrections.

## Confidence Drill

Say this daily:

> I do not need perfect English to communicate. I need clear English, honest practice, and regular correction.
