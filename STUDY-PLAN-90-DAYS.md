# 90-Day English Study Plan

## Days 1-30: Foundation

Focus: sounds, basic grammar, simple sentences, and daily speaking.

- Study English sounds and basic pronunciation.
- Learn parts of speech, nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and articles.
- Write simple sentences every day.
- Speak about daily life for 1 minute every day.
- Correct common Indian English mistakes.

Weekly output:

- 25 corrected sentences
- 20 vocabulary words
- 5 short speaking recordings
- 1 paragraph

## Days 31-60: Structure

Focus: tenses, clauses, paragraphs, pronunciation drills, and professional vocabulary.

- Study tenses and subject-verb agreement.
- Learn sentence structure, phrases, clauses, and conditionals.
- Practice word stress, sentence stress, rhythm, and intonation.
- Write work updates, email replies, and short explanations.
- Build professional vocabulary for meetings and interviews.

Weekly output:

- 40 corrected sentences
- 2 paragraphs
- 2 worknote examples
- 5 pronunciation drills
- 2 interview answers

## Days 61-90: Fluency

Focus: speaking, writing, interviews, public speaking, and professional communication.

- Practice daily speaking without memorizing.
- Write professional emails and ticket updates.
- Explain technical issues simply.
- Practice interview answers.
- Give short public speaking talks.

Weekly output:

- 3 recorded speaking answers
- 2 professional emails
- 2 technical explanations
- 1 public speaking script
- 1 weekly mistake review
