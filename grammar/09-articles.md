# Articles

## 1. Why this matters

Articles point to nouns: a, an, and the. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Articles is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Articles teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- a ticket
- an issue
- the customer
- the Azure subscription

## 6. Common mistakes

- I have doubt. -> I have a question.
- Please share logs. -> Please share the logs.
- He is engineer. -> He is an engineer.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I have doubt.
  Correct: I have a question.
- Incorrect: Please share logs.
  Correct: Please share the logs.
- Incorrect: He is engineer.
  Correct: He is an engineer.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
