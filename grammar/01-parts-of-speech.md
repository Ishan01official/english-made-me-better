# Parts Of Speech

## 1. Why this matters

Parts of speech show the job of each word. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Parts Of Speech is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Parts Of Speech teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Noun: server
- Verb: restart
- Adjective: urgent
- Adverb: quickly

## 6. Common mistakes

- I quickly issue fixed. -> I fixed the issue quickly.
- The quickly server. -> The fast server.
- She response. -> She responds.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I quickly issue fixed.
  Correct: I fixed the issue quickly.
- Incorrect: The quickly server.
  Correct: The fast server.
- Incorrect: She response.
  Correct: She responds.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Name the word roles: The engineer checked the logs carefully.
2. Write one noun, one verb, and one adjective.
3. Correct: The quick resolved issue.

## 10. Answer key

1. engineer = noun, checked = verb, logs = noun, carefully = adverb.
2. Example: ticket, resolve, clear.
3. The engineer resolved the issue quickly.
