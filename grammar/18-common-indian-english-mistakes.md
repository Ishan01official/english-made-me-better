# Common Indian English Mistakes

## 1. Why this matters

Some phrases are common in Indian workplaces but sound unnatural or unclear globally. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Common Indian English Mistakes is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Common Indian English Mistakes teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I have a question.
- Please reply.
- I completed it this morning.
- Please take the required action.

## 6. Common mistakes

- I am having a doubt. -> I have a question.
- Please revert back. -> Please reply.
- Today morning I completed it. -> I completed it this morning.
- Kindly do the needful. -> Please take the required action.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I am having a doubt.
  Correct: I have a question.
- Incorrect: Please revert back.
  Correct: Please reply.
- Incorrect: Today morning I completed it.
  Correct: I completed it this morning.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice
