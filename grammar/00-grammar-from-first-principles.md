# Grammar From First Principles

## 1. Why this matters

Grammar gives structure to meaning. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Grammar From First Principles is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Grammar From First Principles teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I work.
- She is learning.
- We solved the issue yesterday.

## 6. Common mistakes

- He do not know. -> He does not know.
- I has a question. -> I have a question.
- Discuss about the issue. -> Discuss the issue.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: He do not know.
  Correct: He does not know.
- Incorrect: I has a question.
  Correct: I have a question.
- Incorrect: Discuss about the issue.
  Correct: Discuss the issue.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Write the parts of this sentence: The team fixed the issue quickly.
2. Correct: She have a meeting.
3. Write one clear sentence about your work.

## 10. Answer key

1. The/team = subject, fixed = verb, the issue = object, quickly = adverb.
2. She has a meeting.
3. Example: I updated the ticket today.
