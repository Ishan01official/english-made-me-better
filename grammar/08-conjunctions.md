# Conjunctions

## 1. Why this matters

Conjunctions connect words, phrases, and clauses. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Conjunctions is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Conjunctions teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I checked the logs and updated the ticket.
- The server is slow because CPU usage is high.
- I can join now or later.

## 6. Common mistakes

- I was late because of traffic was heavy. -> I was late because traffic was heavy.
- Although it failed but we retried. -> Although it failed, we retried.
- He joined and he left early. -> He joined, but he left early.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I was late because of traffic was heavy.
  Correct: I was late because traffic was heavy.
- Incorrect: Although it failed but we retried.
  Correct: Although it failed, we retried.
- Incorrect: He joined and he left early.
  Correct: He joined, but he left early.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Join with because: The build failed. A test failed.
2. Correct: Although I tried but it failed.
3. Write one sentence with 'and'.

## 10. Answer key

1. The build failed because a test failed.
2. Although I tried, it failed.
3. I checked the issue and updated the ticket.
