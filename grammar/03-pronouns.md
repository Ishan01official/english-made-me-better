# Pronouns

## 1. Why this matters

Pronouns replace nouns to avoid repetition. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Pronouns is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Pronouns teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I checked the logs.
- She replied to the email.
- They joined the call.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Me checked it. -> I checked it.
- Him is ready. -> He is ready.
- This is between you and I. -> This is between you and me.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Me checked it.
  Correct: I checked it.
- Incorrect: Him is ready.
  Correct: He is ready.
- Incorrect: This is between you and I.
  Correct: This is between you and me.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice
