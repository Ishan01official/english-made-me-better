# Adverbs

## 1. Why this matters

Adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Adverbs is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Adverbs teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- She speaks clearly.
- The issue was resolved quickly.
- He is very helpful.

## 6. Common mistakes

- She speaks clear. -> She speaks clearly.
- I completed it quick. -> I completed it quickly.
- He works very hardly. -> He works very hard.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: She speaks clear.
  Correct: She speaks clearly.
- Incorrect: I completed it quick.
  Correct: I completed it quickly.
- Incorrect: He works very hardly.
  Correct: He works very hard.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
