# Nouns

## 1. Why this matters

Nouns name people, places, things, and ideas. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Nouns is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Nouns teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- The developer fixed the bug.
- Confidence grows with practice.
- The meeting starts at 10 AM.

## 6. Common mistakes

- I need an information. -> I need information.
- She gave many advices. -> She gave much advice.
- The equipments are ready. -> The equipment is ready.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I need an information.
  Correct: I need information.
- Incorrect: She gave many advices.
  Correct: She gave much advice.
- Incorrect: The equipments are ready.
  Correct: The equipment is ready.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Find nouns: The customer shared the logs.
2. Correct: I received many feedbacks.
3. Write 3 work-related nouns.
