# Verbs

## 1. Why this matters

Verbs show action, state, or time. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Verbs is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Verbs teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I work remotely.
- She is confident.
- They completed the update.
- Main verb: I checked the logs.
- Helping verb: I have checked the logs.
- Helping verb + main verb: She is reviewing the ticket.

## 6. Common mistakes

- He do the task. -> He does the task.
- Did you went? -> Did you go?
- She have access. -> She has access.
- He does not knows. -> He does not know.
- I am completed it. -> I completed it.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: He do the task.
  Correct: He does the task.
- Incorrect: Did you went?
  Correct: Did you go?
- Incorrect: She have access.
  Correct: She has access.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
