# Tenses

## 1. Why this matters

Tenses show when an action happens. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Tenses is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Tenses teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- I work every day.
- I worked yesterday.
- I will work tomorrow.
- I am working now.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Yesterday I complete it. -> Yesterday I completed it.
- I am knowing the answer. -> I know the answer.
- Did you completed it? -> Did you complete it?

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Yesterday I complete it.
  Correct: Yesterday I completed it.
- Incorrect: I am knowing the answer.
  Correct: I know the answer.
- Incorrect: Did you completed it?
  Correct: Did you complete it?

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
