# Phrases And Clauses

## 1. Why this matters

Phrases are word groups without a full subject-verb idea. Clauses have a subject and verb. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Phrases And Clauses is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Phrases And Clauses teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- after the meeting
- because the server failed
- the issue that you reported

## 6. Common mistakes

- Because of I was busy. -> Because I was busy.
- The ticket which assigned to me. -> The ticket was assigned to me.
- After completed the task. -> After I completed the task.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Because of I was busy.
  Correct: Because I was busy.
- Incorrect: The ticket which assigned to me.
  Correct: The ticket was assigned to me.
- Incorrect: After completed the task.
  Correct: After I completed the task.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
