# Punctuation

## 1. Why this matters

Punctuation helps readers hear pauses and understand structure. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Punctuation is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Punctuation teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Please share the logs.
- Can you confirm the subscription ID?
- I checked the logs, but I need more details.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Please share the logs -> Please share the logs.
- Can you confirm. -> Can you confirm?
- I checked logs but need access. -> I checked the logs, but I need access.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Please share the logs
  Correct: Please share the logs.
- Incorrect: Can you confirm.
  Correct: Can you confirm?
- Incorrect: I checked logs but need access.
  Correct: I checked the logs, but I need access.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
