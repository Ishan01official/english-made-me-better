# Adjectives

## 1. Why this matters

Adjectives describe nouns. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Adjectives is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Adjectives teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- a clear update
- an urgent issue
- a helpful answer

## 6. Common mistakes

- This is a importance task. -> This is an important task.
- The issue is urgency. -> The issue is urgent.
- A more better option. -> A better option.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: This is a importance task.
  Correct: This is an important task.
- Incorrect: The issue is urgency.
  Correct: The issue is urgent.
- Incorrect: A more better option.
  Correct: A better option.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
