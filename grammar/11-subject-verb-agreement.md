# Subject-Verb Agreement

## 1. Why this matters

The verb must match the subject. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Subject-Verb Agreement is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Subject-Verb Agreement teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- He works.
- They work.
- The server runs.
- The logs show errors.

## 6. Common mistakes

- He do not know. -> He does not know.
- The team are ready. -> The team is ready.
- The logs shows errors. -> The logs show errors.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: He do not know.
  Correct: He does not know.
- Incorrect: The team are ready.
  Correct: The team is ready.
- Incorrect: The logs shows errors.
  Correct: The logs show errors.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Correct: She have access.
2. Choose: The issue is/are fixed.
3. Write 3 he/she/it sentences.

## 10. Answer key

1. She has access.
2. The issue is fixed.
3. Example: He checks. She replies. It works.
