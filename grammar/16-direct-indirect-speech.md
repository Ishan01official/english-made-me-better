# Direct And Indirect Speech

## 1. Why this matters

Direct speech quotes exact words. Indirect speech reports the meaning. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Direct And Indirect Speech is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Direct And Indirect Speech teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Direct: He said, 'I need access.'
- Indirect: He said that he needed access.
- Direct: She asked, 'Can you join?'
- Indirect: She asked if I could join.

## 6. Common mistakes

- He said me to join. -> He told me to join.
- She asked that can I join. -> She asked if I could join.
- He told that he is busy. -> He said that he was busy.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: He said me to join.
  Correct: He told me to join.
- Incorrect: She asked that can I join.
  Correct: She asked if I could join.
- Incorrect: He told that he is busy.
  Correct: He said that he was busy.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Change: He said, 'I am busy.'
2. Correct: She asked that can you help.
3. Write one direct and indirect example.

## 10. Answer key

1. He said that he was busy.
2. She asked if I could help.
3. Example: Direct: He said, 'I checked it.' Indirect: He said that he had checked it.
