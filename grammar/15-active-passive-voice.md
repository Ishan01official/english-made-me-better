# Active And Passive Voice

## 1. Why this matters

Voice changes focus: who does the action or what receives the action. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Active And Passive Voice is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Active And Passive Voice teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Active: I resolved the issue.
- Passive: The issue was resolved.
- Active is usually clearer for work updates.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Issue resolved by me. -> I resolved the issue.
- It was did yesterday. -> It was done yesterday.
- The logs checked by team. -> The team checked the logs.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Issue resolved by me.
  Correct: I resolved the issue.
- Incorrect: It was did yesterday.
  Correct: It was done yesterday.
- Incorrect: The logs checked by team.
  Correct: The team checked the logs.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.

## 9. Practice

1. Change to active: The ticket was updated by me.
2. Choose clearer: I fixed it / It was fixed by me.
3. Write one passive sentence.

## 10. Answer key

1. I updated the ticket.
2. I fixed it.
3. The issue was resolved.
