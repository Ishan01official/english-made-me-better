# Prepositions

## 1. Why this matters

Prepositions show relationship: place, time, direction, and connection. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Prepositions is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Prepositions teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- in the meeting
- on Monday
- at 5 PM
- for the customer

## 6. Common mistakes

- Discuss about the issue. -> Discuss the issue.
- I am in call. -> I am on a call.
- I will update in Monday. -> I will update on Monday.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Discuss about the issue.
  Correct: Discuss the issue.
- Incorrect: I am in call.
  Correct: I am on a call.
- Incorrect: I will update in Monday.
  Correct: I will update on Monday.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

No major difference for normal daily use. Use one style consistently.
