# Short Vowels

## 1. Why this matters

Short vowels help listeners separate words like ship and sheep, full and fool, live and leave.

## 2. First principles explanation

A vowel is the center sound of a syllable. A short vowel is quick and relaxed. Do not stretch it.

## 3. Simple explanation

Short vowels are small sounds. Say them quickly and clearly.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a short vowel like a quick tap. Do not hold it for too long.

## 5. Real examples

- ship
- bit
- full
- bed
- cup

## 6. Common mistakes

- ship -> sheep
- bit -> beat
- full -> fool

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: I need a sheep update.
  Correct: I need a ship update. 
- Incorrect: This is a beat confusing.
  Correct: This is a bit confusing.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

The exact vowel quality can change by accent, but the short/long contrast is important in both.

## 9. Practice

1. Say: ship, bit, full, bed, cup.
2. Say each word in a sentence.
3. Record and check if the vowel is too long.

## 10. Answer key

1. The vowel should be short.
2. The word should not sound stretched.
3. Repeat slowly, then naturally.
