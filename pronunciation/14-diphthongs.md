# Diphthongs

## 1. Why this matters

Diphthongs are moving vowel sounds. They make words like day, go, now, and boy clear.

## 2. First principles explanation

A diphthong starts with one vowel position and moves to another vowel position in the same syllable.

## 3. Simple explanation

Your mouth moves during the vowel.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

A diphthong is a vowel that travels from one sound to another.

## 5. Real examples

- day
- go
- now
- boy
- either
- route

## 6. Common mistakes

- Making day too flat.
- Saying go without the ending movement.
- Saying now like a single plain vowel.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: deh-ta for data when you mean day-ta.
  Correct: day-ta.
- Incorrect: go with no mouth movement.
  Correct: go with a small movement.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Some diphthongs differ by accent, especially in words like either, neither, and route.

## 9. Practice

1. Say: day, data.
2. Say: go, though.
3. Say: now, route.

## 10. Answer key

1. The vowel should move.
2. Do not freeze the mouth.
3. Keep the word natural, not exaggerated.
