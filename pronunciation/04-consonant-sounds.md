# Consonant Sounds

## 1. Why this matters

Consonants shape the edges of words. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Consonant Sounds is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Consonant Sounds teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- thought starts with /θ/
- world starts with /w/
- through starts with /θr/

## 6. Common mistakes

- Saying /t/ for /θ/. -> Put tongue lightly between teeth.
- Dropping final consonants. -> Finish the word.
- Adding vowel after final consonant. -> Say work, not work-uh.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Saying /t/ for /θ/.
  Correct: Put tongue lightly between teeth.
- Incorrect: Dropping final consonants.
  Correct: Finish the word.
- Incorrect: Adding vowel after final consonant.
  Correct: Say work, not work-uh.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.
