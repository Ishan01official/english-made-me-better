# English Sounds

## 1. Why this matters

English spelling is not always sound. Sounds are the real building blocks. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

English Sounds is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. English Sounds teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- water
- better
- thought
- though
- through

## 6. Common mistakes

- Pronouncing thought and though the same. -> Practice final /t/ in thought.
- Adding extra vowel sounds. -> Keep consonant clusters tight.
- Ignoring /w/ in world. -> Start with rounded lips.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Pronouncing thought and though the same.
  Correct: Practice final /t/ in thought.
- Incorrect: Adding extra vowel sounds.
  Correct: Keep consonant clusters tight.
- Incorrect: Ignoring /w/ in world.
  Correct: Start with rounded lips.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.

## 9. Practice

1. Read: water, better, data.
2. Separate: thought, though, through.
3. Say: world, girl, work.

## 10. Answer key

1. water, better, data.
2. thought has final /t/; though does not; through starts with /thr/.
3. Practice slowly first.
