# Silent Letters

## 1. Why this matters

English spelling often includes letters we do not pronounce. Knowing this improves clarity.

## 2. First principles explanation

Spelling is history. Pronunciation is sound. Some written letters are silent in speech.

## 3. Simple explanation

Do not pronounce every letter you see.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Some letters are written but quiet.

## 5. Real examples

- know
- doubt
- receipt
- talk
- comfortable

## 6. Common mistakes

- Pronouncing the k in know.
- Pronouncing the b in doubt.
- Over-pronouncing every letter in comfortable.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: k-now.
  Correct: know.
- Incorrect: dou-b-t.
  Correct: doubt.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Silent letters are usually similar in American and British English, though surrounding vowel sounds may differ.
