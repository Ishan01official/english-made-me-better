# Indian Speaker Common Problems

## 1. Why this matters

Many Indian speakers are clear in meaning but need work on stress, vowel contrast, and consonant clusters. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Indian Speaker Common Problems is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Indian Speaker Common Problems teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- world
- girl
- work
- thought
- through

## 6. Common mistakes

- Adding extra vowel: world-uh. -> Say world.
- Saying v for w. -> Round lips for w.
- Flat stress. -> Stress important syllables.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Adding extra vowel: world-uh.
  Correct: Say world.
- Incorrect: Saying v for w.
  Correct: Round lips for w.
- Incorrect: Flat stress.
  Correct: Stress important syllables.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.

## 9. Practice

1. Say: world, girl, work.
2. Say: thought, though, through.
3. Record: Please share the required logs.

## 10. Answer key

1. Keep final consonants tight.
2. Separate the three words.
3. Check stress and clarity.

## 12. Daily drill

Write 5 original sentences. Read them aloud. Correct one mistake. Then teach the rule in 3 simple lines.
