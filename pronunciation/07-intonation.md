# Intonation

## 1. Why this matters

Intonation is the rise and fall of your voice. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Intonation is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Intonation teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Questions often rise or fall depending on type.
- Statements usually fall.
- Lists rise before the final item.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Using a flat voice always. -> Add natural movement.
- Rising every statement. -> End statements with confidence.
- Sounding unsure in updates. -> Use falling intonation.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Using a flat voice always.
  Correct: Add natural movement.
- Incorrect: Rising every statement.
  Correct: End statements with confidence.
- Incorrect: Sounding unsure in updates.
  Correct: Use falling intonation.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.

## 9. Practice

1. Say: Can you confirm?
2. Say: The issue is resolved.
3. Say a three-item list.

## 10. Answer key

1. Yes/no questions may rise.
2. Statement falls.
3. Rise, rise, fall.
