# Word Stress

## 1. Why this matters

One syllable in a word is stronger. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Word Stress is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Word Stress teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- de-VEL-op-ment
- com-FORT-a-ble
- ad-VER-tise-ment or ad-ver-TISE-ment

## 6. Common mistakes

- Stressing every syllable equally. -> Make one syllable stronger.
- Wrong stress in development. -> Stress VEL.
- Over-pronouncing comfortable. -> Keep it smooth.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Stressing every syllable equally.
  Correct: Make one syllable stronger.
- Incorrect: Wrong stress in development.
  Correct: Stress VEL.
- Incorrect: Over-pronouncing comfortable.
  Correct: Keep it smooth.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.

## 9. Practice

1. Mark stress: development.
2. Say comfortable naturally.
3. Say advertisement in US and UK styles.

## 10. Answer key

1. de-VEL-op-ment.
2. COMF-ter-bul or COM-fuh-tuh-bul.
3. US often AD-ver-tize-ment; UK often ad-VER-tis-ment.

## 11. Feynman exercise

Explain this topic in your own simple words.

Use this frame:

- The topic is...

## 12. Daily drill

Write 5 original sentences. Read them aloud. Correct one mistake. Then teach the rule in 3 simple lines.
