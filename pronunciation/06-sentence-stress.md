# Sentence Stress

## 1. Why this matters

Important words get stronger stress in a sentence. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Sentence Stress is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Sentence Stress teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Please SHARE the LOGS.
- I NEED more DETAILS.
- The ISSUE is FIXED.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Stressing every word. -> Stress content words.
- Speaking too flat. -> Raise important words.
- Stressing grammar words too much. -> Keep small words light.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Stressing every word.
  Correct: Stress content words.
- Incorrect: Speaking too flat.
  Correct: Raise important words.
- Incorrect: Stressing grammar words too much.
  Correct: Keep small words light.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.

## 9. Practice

1. Mark stress: Please share the required logs.
2. Say: We do not have access.
3. Record one work update.

## 10. Answer key

1. SHARE, REQUIRED, LOGS.
2. WE, NOT, HAVE, ACCESS.
3. Listen and repeat.

## 11. Feynman exercise

Explain this topic in your own simple words.

Use this frame:

- The topic is...
- It matters because...
- A simple example is...
- One mistake I must avoid is...

## 12. Daily drill

Write 5 original sentences. Read them aloud. Correct one mistake. Then teach the rule in 3 simple lines.
