# Connected Speech

## 1. Why this matters

Native speech connects words instead of separating every word. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Connected Speech is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Connected Speech teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- want to -> wanna in casual speech
- going to -> gonna in casual speech
- next step links /t/ and /s/

## 6. Common mistakes

- Overusing casual reductions at work. -> Use clear professional speech.
- Separating every word too strongly. -> Link naturally.
- Dropping meaning words. -> Keep important words clear.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Overusing casual reductions at work.
  Correct: Use clear professional speech.
- Incorrect: Separating every word too strongly.
  Correct: Link naturally.
- Incorrect: Dropping meaning words.
  Correct: Keep important words clear.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.
