# Pronunciation Word Bank: 150 Drills

Pronunciation improves through slow, accurate repetition.

## First Principles

Pronunciation is physical. You train:

- lips
- tongue
- jaw
- breath
- stress
- rhythm
- intonation

## How To Use This Word Bank

1. Say the word slowly.
2. Say the practice sentence.
3. Record yourself.
4. Listen for the target sound.
5. Repeat naturally.

## Shadowing Drill

Use this pattern:

> Listen -> pause -> repeat -> record -> compare -> repeat

## Indian Speaker Focus

Watch for:

- Extra vowel at the end: work-uh, world-uh.
- Confusing /v/ and /w/.
- Making every syllable equal.
- Pronouncing from spelling instead of sound.
