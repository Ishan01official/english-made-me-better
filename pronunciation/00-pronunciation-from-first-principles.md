# Pronunciation From First Principles

## 1. Why this matters

Pronunciation turns written English into understandable sound. helps you make clear English. When this topic is weak, your listener has to guess your meaning.

## 2. First principles explanation

English starts with meaning. To express meaning, choose the right word role, place it in the right position, and connect it to the rest of the sentence.

## 3. Simple explanation

Pronunciation From First Principles is a tool for building clear sentences. Learn the pattern, see examples, then use it in your own speaking and writing.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

Think of a sentence like a small machine. Each part has a job. Pronunciation From First Principles teaches one job in that machine.

## 5. Real examples

- Sound first, spelling second.
- Stress changes clarity.
- Rhythm makes speech natural.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Speaking every letter equally. -> Stress important syllables.
- Reading silently only. -> Practice aloud.
- Ignoring mouth position. -> Notice tongue, lips, and jaw.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: Speaking every letter equally.
  Correct: Stress important syllables.
- Incorrect: Reading silently only.
  Correct: Practice aloud.
- Incorrect: Ignoring mouth position.
  Correct: Notice tongue, lips, and jaw.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Pronunciation often differs between American and British English. Choose one target accent for drills, but understand both.
