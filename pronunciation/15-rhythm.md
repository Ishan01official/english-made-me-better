# Rhythm

## 1. Why this matters

Rhythm makes English sound natural. It helps listeners follow your main ideas.

## 2. First principles explanation

English rhythm comes from stressed content words and lighter grammar words.

## 3. Simple explanation

Important words are stronger. Small grammar words are lighter.

## 4. Explain like I am 12

English has beats like music. Important words get the beat.

## 5. Real examples

- Please SHARE the LOGS.
- I NEED more DETAILS.
- The ISSUE is RESOLVED.

## 6. Common mistakes

- Stressing every word equally.
- Speaking too fast.
- Not pausing between ideas.

## 7. Correct vs incorrect

- Incorrect: PLEASE SHARE THE REQUIRED LOGS with equal stress.
  Correct: Please SHARE the required LOGS.

## 8. American vs British difference, if applicable

Both accents use stress rhythm. Intonation patterns can differ, but clear rhythm matters in both.

## 9. Practice

1. Mark the strong words in a sentence.
2. Read the sentence slowly.
3. Read it naturally.

## 10. Answer key

1. Stress meaning words.
2. Keep grammar words lighter.
3. Pause after complete ideas.

## 12. Daily drill
